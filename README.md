# Audiograph

Consent-gated, end-to-end encrypted voice memory.

> Bump to consent. Seal at origin. Carry on XMTP. Pin on Koii. Remember in private compute.

**Read the full charter: [AUDIOGRAPH.md](AUDIOGRAPH.md)**

---

An audiograph is a captured moment of human speech: encrypted end-to-end at the moment of creation, consent-gated by a physical pairing between the people present, transported on open XMTP relays, pinned as ciphertext by Koii node operators who are rewarded for availability and can never read what they hold, and made comprehensible only inside private compute.

Two rights, held together: **the freedom of speech, and the ability to record that speech.**

Lineage: [Mitosis Labs](https://github.com/mitosislabs) private-superintelligence thesis and Cortex · [Koii Network](https://github.com/koii-network) substrate · [XMTP](https://github.com/xmtp) open encrypted transport.
